AutopostInG — Privacy Policy

AutopostInG is a personal tool used only by its owner with the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Data we access: the TikTok display name of the connected account (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How data is stored: access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

Data we do not collect: we do not collect data of other TikTok users, followers, viewers or any personal information beyond what is listed above.

Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services). Local tokens are deleted together with the app files.

Contact: the developer account email.
